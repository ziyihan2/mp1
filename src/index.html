Story

🍔

Before I met Ziyi Han, I briefly lived with another family when I was only a few months old. Then one day, they had to move and, for reasons that were never properly explained to me, I could not go with them. Very suspicious. So I was sent to an animal shelter. Life there was mostly sleeping, eating, and judging people from inside my cage. Then one day, a shelter worker suddenly picked me up and carried me into a small room. Two humans were waiting there. Naturally, I inspected them carefully. They seemed acceptable, so I allowed them to play with me for a while.
Then I was taken back to my cage. Excuse me?

I had no idea what that meeting was supposed to mean.

Unfortunately, before I could investigate further, I got sick. For the next few days, my schedule became extremely unpleasant: medicine, injections, more medicine, and absolutely no respect for my personal space. Somehow, I survived this terrible injustice. A few days later, I was taken out of my cage again. This time, they put me inside a carrier and took me somewhere completely new. And there they were again — the humans from that mysterious little room. Apparently, I had been adopted.

Since then, life has improved dramatically. I have unlimited sleeping locations, regular meals, snacks, windows for bird surveillance, and a human who handles most of my responsibilities for me.

These days, my schedule is very demanding: eat, nap, move to another location, nap again, watch some birds, request snacks, supervise Ziyi, and then recover from all that hard work with another nap.

I think things worked out pretty well.🐱
